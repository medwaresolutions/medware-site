How I work

Strategy and portfolio

Use case identification and prioritisation, business cases, ROI definition and tracking, build versus buy, and the discipline of stopping work that is not returning.

Governance and responsible AI

Model oversight, bias and transparency, data protection and sovereignty by design, and regulatory alignment under TGA and AHPRA.

Change and enablement

Executive briefing, board reporting, AI literacy programmes and workflow redesign. Prompt training does not change behaviour. Workflow redesign does.

I am fluent enough in the technology to specify it, evaluate it and hold vendors and internal teams accountable for outcomes. I am not a software engineer by trade, and I do not present as one. The value is in knowing which questions to ask.
